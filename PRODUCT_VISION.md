# DecisionGap OS - Complete Product Vision (target architecture)

**The ideal system goes beyond this deployed V1.** It is a merchant-controlled operating system for discovering why customers hesitate, selecting appropriate interventions, engaging customers through AI sales agents, measuring incremental profitable outcomes, and learning which interventions work for different contexts.

## Target modules

1. Unified merchant workspace (multi-tenant SaaS, organizations, user roles, SSO, audit history).
2. Customer evidence connectors (Shopify, WooCommerce, Stripe, CRM, ticket systems, surveys; privacy and retention controls).
3. Decision-barrier intelligence (themes, competing hypotheses, confidence, source lineage, counterevidence).
4. Sales strategy planning (playbooks constrained by merchant pricing, margin, stock, customer consent, brand rules).
5. Omni-channel AI sales execution (web chat, consented email and messaging channels, human handoff, verified knowledge retrieval).
6. Autonomous experiment management (randomized audience allocation, pre-registered metrics, holdouts, power, lifecycle and rollback).
7. Commerce actions (product recommendations, secure carts, real checkout handoff, returns support, permitted offers).
8. Customer trust controls (AI disclosure, truthfulness, anti-manipulation, opt-outs, transparent receipts).
9. Business performance (gross margin, cohort analyses, returns, customer satisfaction and causal incremental lift).
10. Governance (fine-grained approvals, rate limits, budget caps, tool permissions, security and regulatory controls).

## Ideal north-star outcome

Increase **incremental contribution profit with customer welfare guardrails**, not raw clicks or conversion regardless of consequences. Do not optimize for vulnerable customer exploitation or unapproved contact. Distinguish correlation, attribution and causal incrementality.

## Current implementation boundary

V1 includes an operational single-merchant cloud MVP, an AI sales assistant, evidence-linked playbooks and manual approval records, and optional Shopify Storefront checkout. It does NOT include automated email/campaign delivery, automatic price modifications, multi-tenant identity, billing, real A/B experiment deployment, full CRM integrations, enterprise compliance, or proven incremental-lift conclusions.
